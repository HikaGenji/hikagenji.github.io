KINGSFALL

  1. Build. Spend your rupees stacking stone, brick, wood and roof tiles around your king. Unsupported pieces fall.
  2. Fire. Take turns lobbing cannonballs. Gravity pulls, the wind pushes, and the flags show which way it blows.
