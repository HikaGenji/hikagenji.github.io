KINGSFALL 3D

  1. Build. Both kingdoms start in the Stone Age with 300 gold. Stack walls, hire soldiers and sink gold mines. Gold you don't spend stays in your treasury.
  2. Each turn, do one thing. Fire one soldier's weapon, put gold into research, or send everyone to dig for gold.
  3. Advance through time. Finish the research and your whole kingdom jumps to the next era: new walls, new uniforms, a new weapon and a new look for the king.
  4. Mines pay every turn, and pay more each era. The enemy can blow them up.
  5. Win by emptying the other king's hearts, or by killing all of his soldiers.
