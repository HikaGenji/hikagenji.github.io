KINGSFALL 3D

  1. Build. Spend 260 rupees stacking stone, brick, wood, battlements and turret roofs around your king. Tap a block's face to build onto it. Unsupported pieces fall.
  2. Fire. Turn, raise and charge your cannon. Gravity pulls, the wind pushes sideways too, and the flags and arrow show where it blows.
  3. Topple. Each king has six hearts. Walls soften the blast. Empty the other king's hearts to win.
